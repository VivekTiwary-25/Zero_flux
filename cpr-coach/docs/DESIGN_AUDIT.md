# CPR Coach design audit

**Method:** source-only review of `docs/SPEC.md` sections 1, 3, and 5; every file under `app/src/main/java/com/zeroflux/cprcoach/ui/`; `AppRoot.kt`; and `PractiseHost.kt`. No one has seen this UI on a phone. Rendered contrast, clipping, reachability, actual animation, and device-specific layout therefore remain unverified.

## Failures, ranked by likely judge-visible impact

| Rank | Rule | Screen / evidence | Result | Smallest fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Nothing animates at rest | All screens with the mic: `ui/MicButton.kt:62-69,72-84`; placements include `ui/HomeScreen.kt:201-207`, `PractiseHost.kt:140-145`, `ui/CameraOverlay.kt:77-82`, and `ui/EmergencyScreen.kt:137-139`. | **Fail.** A looping ring animation continues for the full listening state. | Keep a static listening ring; animate only the transition into listening. |
| 2 | Readable contrast / camera overlays | Practise camera: `ui/Theme.kt:35-36,52`; `ui/CameraOverlay.kt:237-259,263-280`. Secondary text and feedback appear over a 60%-opaque scrim on an unrestricted live preview. | **Fail to guarantee contrast.** A bright preview can compromise critical status/readout legibility; actual contrast was not measured. | Use a darker or opaque backing behind critical readouts and status, retaining flat overlays. |
| 3 | One meaning per colour; teal means good / in-range / ready | Home language chip `ui/HomeScreen.kt:248-255`; mic `ui/MicButton.kt:76-87`; walkthrough Next/illustrations `ui/WalkthroughScreen.kt:161-163,194-216`; scorecard Swap roles `ui/ScorecardScreen.kt:128-139`. Compare legitimate readiness/range uses: `ui/CameraOverlay.kt:108-109,232-235,266-280`; `ui/ScorecardScreen.kt:108-115`. | **Fail.** Teal also communicates selection, active listening, navigation, and decoration. | Reserve teal for ready/good feedback; make those controls and illustrations neutral. |
| 4 | One meaning per colour; amber means needs correcting | Practise camera paused state: `ui/CameraOverlay.kt:252-256`; correction uses include `ui/CameraOverlay.kt:232-235,266-280`. | **Fail.** A paused state is not necessarily something to correct. | Use neutral styling for paused status. |
| 5 | Respect reduced motion | Walkthrough: `ui/WalkthroughScreen.kt:59-67`; camera timer: `ui/CameraOverlay.kt:284-311`; shared clay buttons: `ui/Theme.kt:85-92`. In contrast, Home press motion `ui/HomeScreen.kt:71-85`, mic `ui/MicButton.kt:142-163`, camera edge `ui/CameraOverlay.kt:326-375`, and scorecard entrance `ui/ScorecardScreen.kt:44-59` check animator settings. | **Fail at the source-level safeguard.** The slide transition, timer tween, and clay press spring have no explicit reduced-motion branch. Whether platform scaling suppresses them in practice was not tested. | Apply the existing motion preference to use immediate page changes, timer updates, and pressed-state changes. |
