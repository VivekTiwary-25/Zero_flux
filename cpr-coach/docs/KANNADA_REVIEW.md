## 2. Key Findings & Detailed Analysis

### A. Critical Medical Safety Risks
1. **`slow_down` (`ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ನಿಧಾನ ಮಾಡಿ` → `ವೇಗ ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ಕಡಿಮೆ ಮಾಡಿ`)**: In clinical resuscitation guidelines, high compression rates (>120 bpm) exhaust rescuers and reduce ventricular filling, but compression *depth* (>50 mm) must be strictly maintained. In conversational Kannada, telling someone "ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ನಿಧಾನ ಮಾಡಿ" is the standard idiom for "take it easy / do it gently". If a rescuer presses gently, cardiac perfusion pressure drops immediately. Explicitly specifying `ವೇಗ ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ಕಡಿಮೆ ಮಾಡಿ` (reduce speed slightly) or `ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ನಿಧಾನವಾಗಿ ಒತ್ತಿ` protects compression depth.
2. **`score_pauses` (`ಬಿಡುವುಗಳು` → `ತಡೆಗಳು` / `ವಿರಾಮಗಳು`)**: "ಬಿಡುವು" translates to leisure time or recreation. Showing "ಬಿಡುವುಗಳು" on a CPR scorecard suggests the rescuer was taking pleasant breaks, obscuring the clinical gravity of hands-off intervals during cardiac arrest.
3. **`em_step_hands` (`ಎದೆಯ ಮಧ್ಯದಲ್ಲಿ ಕೈ ಇಡಿ` → `ಎದೆಯ ಮಧ್ಯದಲ್ಲಿ ಎರಡೂ ಕೈಗಳನ್ನು ಇಡಿ`)**: One-handed compressions generate insufficient force in adult CPR. Using the explicit plural `ಎರಡೂ ಕೈಗಳನ್ನು` prevents confusion during high-stress emergencies.

### B. UI Controls & Semantic Collisions
1. **`live_pause` vs. `live_stop` (`ನಿಲ್ಲಿಸಿ` vs. `ಮುಗಿಸಿ`)**:
   - `live_pause` currently uses `ನಿಲ್ಲಿಸಿ` (literally "Stop").
   - `live_stop` currently uses `ಮುಗಿಸಿ` (literally "Finish").
   - `commands.stop` uses `ನಿಲ್ಲಿಸಿ`.
   - On the live camera screen, the user sees a Pause button labeled "ನಿಲ್ಲಿಸಿ" adjacent to a Stop button labeled "ಮುಗಿಸಿ". Tapping "ನಿಲ್ಲಿಸಿ" pauses the session; shouting "ನಿಲ್ಲಿಸಿ" aborts the session. Changing Pause to `ವಿರಾಮ` (the standard Android Kannada localization term for Pause) and Stop to `ನಿಲ್ಲಿಸಿ` resolves this contradiction.

### C. Voice Synthesis & Audio Engine Constraints
1. **`countdown` (`ಮೂರು, ಎರಡು, ಒಂದು, ಶುರು` → `ಶುರುವಾಗುತ್ತಿದೆ: ಮೂರು, ಎರಡು, ಒಂದು`)**: `PractiseSession.kt` hardcodes a 3-second countdown window (`countdownSeconds = 3`), starting the metronome at $t = 3.0\text{ s}$. Placing "ಶುರು" at the end of the clip means speech extends past the start of the metronome. Placing the verb first (`ಶುರುವಾಗುತ್ತಿದೆ...`) allows the clip to conclude on "ಒಂದು", transitioning seamlessly into the metronome clicks.
2. **`em_call` spacing (`ಒಂದು ಒಂದು ಎರಡು ಕ್ಕೆ` → `ಒಂದು ಒಂದು ಎರಡಕ್ಕೆ`)**: A detached suffix `-ಕ್ಕೆ` causes Meta MMS TTS to pronounce a disjointed "kke" sound. Grammatically joined `ಎರಡಕ್ಕೆ` produces clean, fluid speech.
3. **`em_push` duration**: The Kannada clip currently runs for 4.76 seconds (compared to 1.64 seconds for English). Eliminating conjunction "ಮತ್ತು" and using `ತಾಳಕ್ಕೆ ತಕ್ಕಂತೆ ಬಲವಾಗಿ, ವೇಗವಾಗಿ ಒತ್ತಿ` reduces clip length while improving clarity.
