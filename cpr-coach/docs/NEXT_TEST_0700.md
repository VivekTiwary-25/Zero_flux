# Short phone test (CPH2467, about 4 minutes)

For build **e1b489f** (APK sha1 `3779864a6984`). Two people run one Practise session: the rescuer pushes a firm cushion, the holder holds the phone and calls the time from a stopwatch. Never push on a person.

## Before you start
- The build is installed and the app was started from the PC with picture capture on. Don't close or restart the app; if it closes, plug the phone in and say so.
- **Mic button OFF.** While the mic listens, every spoken line is silenced.
- Phone landscape, side-on to the rescuer, whole body in view, steady.
- The first straight block teaches the app this rescuer's own straight arms and posture. Push properly in it.

## Blocks (the holder calls each change out loud)

| Time | Rescuer does | Screen, within about 3 s | Voice, within about 5 s | OK? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0-25 s | Straight arms, shoulders over hands, with the metronome. **Count pushes.** | Teal, rate shown | "Good. Keep pushing the chest." (about 8 s in) | [ ] |
| 25-40 s | **Elbows clearly bent**, keep pushing | Amber "Straighten arms" | "Keep both elbows straight." | [ ] |
| 40-55 s | Straight again | Back to teal | (nothing new) | [ ] |
| 55-70 s | **Stop.** Hands rest on the cushion, stay still | Rate goes away | "Keep pushing the chest." about 3 s after stopping | [ ] |
| 70-85 s | Straight again | Teal, rate back | (nothing new) | [ ] |
| 85-100 s | **Lean back clearly**: shoulders well behind the hands, arms slanting forward | Amber "Shoulders over hands" | "Shoulders straight over your hands." | [ ] |
| 100-110 s | Straight again | Teal | (nothing new) | [ ] |
| 110-120 s | **Lean far forward**: shoulders past the hands (only tested on made-up frames so far; tell me if nothing happens) | Amber "Shoulders over hands"? | Maybe the same line (the 6 s gap between lines can delay it) | [ ] |
| at 2:00 | Session ends. **Count pushes from 100 s to 110 s.** | Scorecard | "Well done..." | [ ] |

Optional, after the scorecard: tap Emergency (do NOT call anyone; cancel the dialer if it opens), push bent for 10 s, then lean back for 10 s. The big word should change to "Straighten arms", then "Shoulders over hands".

## Send back
- OK / not OK for each row, and roughly how many seconds each change took.
- The two push counts (0-25 s and 100-110 s).
- Whether the skeleton stayed on the real arm, especially in the bent and lean blocks.
- Anything spoken that shouldn't have been, or anything odd.
- Then plug the phone in. I pull the trace and pictures and label them.
