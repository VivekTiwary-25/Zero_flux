# 7-minute phone test (CPH2467)

Two people run one Practise session in under 7 minutes: one rescuer on the cushion, and one holder with a stopwatch calling 15 s blocks. Total run time: 2 min 15 s (9 blocks).

## Rules
- Mic button OFF: listening mutes the coaching voice.
- Phone side-on to the rescuer in landscape orientation.
- Push on a firm cushion; never on a person.
- Picture capture ON: the boss starts it over adb before the run.

## Test blocks (15 s each)
The caller keeps time. Elbow and lean cues play while pushing even without a locked rate number.

| Block | Rescuer action | Holder sees within ~2 s | What should be heard | [ ] |
|---|---|---|---|:---:|
| 1. Straight (0:00–0:15) | Push 100–120 bpm, straight arms, shoulders over hands. Count pushes. | Teal skeleton, teal rate, "Elbows straight" | Metronome (~110 bpm) | [ ] |
| 2. Bent (0:15–0:30) | Continue pushing at pace with deliberately bent elbows | Amber skeleton (<150°), amber "Elbows bent" | "Keep both elbows straight" | [ ] |
| 3. Straight (0:30–0:45) | Straighten arms, resume upright push | Teal skeleton (>160°), teal rate returns | Metronome continues | [ ] |
| 4. Pause (0:45–1:00) | Stop pushing; rest hands on cushion (minor shifting is fine) | Rate clears; skeleton stays visible | "Keep going" spoken after ~2 s | [ ] |
| 5. Straight (1:00–1:15) | Resume pushing with straight arms and shoulders over hands | Teal skeleton, teal rate returns | Metronome continues | [ ] |
| 6. Lean back (1:15–1:30) | Push leaning back so shoulders are behind hands | Amber skeleton, amber "Shoulders over hands" | "Shoulders over hands" | [ ] |
| 7. Straight (1:30–1:45) | Return upright with shoulders over hands | Teal skeleton, amber clears, teal rate | Metronome continues | [ ] |
| 8. Lean forward (1:45–2:00) | Push leaning forward so shoulders are past hands | Amber skeleton, amber "Shoulders over hands" | "Shoulders over hands" | [ ] |
| 9. Straight (2:00–2:15) | Return upright with straight arms. Count pushes. | Teal skeleton, amber clears, teal rate | Metronome continues | [ ] |

## What to send back
- Per-block yes/no: whether each block showed and spoke the expected cues within ~2 s.
- Hand-counted compression counts for block 1 (first straight) and block 9 (last straight).
- Anything odd: missed cues, false triggers, landmark jumps, or audio issues.
