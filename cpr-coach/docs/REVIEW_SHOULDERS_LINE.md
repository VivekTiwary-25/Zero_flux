# Review: “shoulders over hands”

Compared the nine `shoulders_over_hands` drafts in `tools/clipgen/phrases_<lang>.json` with the English line, “Shoulders straight over your hands.” The back-translations below are semantic glosses, not independent native-speaker translations. “Directly above” means vertical alignment, not merely touching or resting on the hands.

| Language | Back-translation | Does it say “directly above”? | Ambiguity / review | Better wording, if needed | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hindi (`hi`) | “Bring your shoulders just above your hands.” | Yes: `ठीक ऊपर` says just/right above. | Clear spatial relation. `हाथों` can refer to hands or arms, though the CPR context points to hands. | Consider `अपने कंधे दोनों हथेलियों के ठीक ऊपर रखें` (“Keep your shoulders just above both palms”) to remove that ambiguity. | High on meaning; medium on suggested phrasing. |
| Bengali (`bn`) | “Keep your shoulders right above your hand(s).” | Yes: `ঠিক উপরে` means right/directly above. | `হাত` can refer to a hand or arm; number is not explicit. | `দুই হাতের তালুর ঠিক উপরে কাঁধ রাখুন` (“Keep your shoulders right above the palms of both hands”). | High on meaning; medium on suggested phrasing. |
| Gujarati (`gu`) | “Keep your shoulders exactly above your hand(s).” | Yes: `બરાબર ઉપર` means exactly/right above. | `હાથ` may mean hand or arm; number is not explicit. | `બંને હથેળીઓની બરાબર ઉપર ખભા રાખો` (“Keep your shoulders exactly above both palms”). | High on meaning; medium on suggested phrasing. |

The main issue is not “above” in most drafts: each explicitly says above, while Kannada is less precise and can be read as “on top of.” Several languages use a word that may mean either hand or arm, so naming both palms would make the target clearer; the proposed alternatives are review candidates, not native-speaker-approved translations. Malayalam and Odia phrase-file wording is not what the app currently speaks because those languages use the English fallback clip. This is a desk review only; a native speaker should confirm naturalness and the CPR posture cue before release.
