Report and one new doc only: cpr-coach/docs/RATE_ALT_METHOD.md, under one page. Do not change the app: other people are editing the engine right now. Run no adb and touch no phone. Give numbers only from your own run, and say plainly what is unknowable without a human count.
Real recordings are on the branch in tools/testdata/: real-cph2467-1..9.csv (real rescuers pushing to a 110/min metronome, about 20 frames a second, pose visibility about 0.95; columns t_ms,wrist_signal,elbow_deg,min_vis,latency_ms,frame_gap_ms,thermal_status,battery_temp_c,side,shoulder_x,shoulder_y,elbow_x,elbow_y,wrist_x,wrist_y,hip_x,hip_y,shoulder_vis,elbow_vis,wrist_vis,hip_vis). They have no human count and include non-pushing stretches. negative-cph2467-nobody.csv is a phone on a desk with nobody pushing: any confident rate from it is false. Today's engine (engine/RateEstimator.kt) gives plausible rates of 100-120 when it fires, but its checks reject most windows mid-push.

This is the hardest open problem in the app. In Python outside this folder, try methods other than the current one on the same recordings: a windowed spectral peak with harmonic check, and peak counting with interval stability, each on 3 to 4 second windows. Compare with a faithful port of engine/RateEstimator.kt on: share of windows with a rate during pushing, agreement over time, time to first rate after motion starts, and false rates in the negative file and on noise. Be honest if the current method is as good. If one is clearly better, give its pseudocode and constants, short enough to port to Kotlin.
