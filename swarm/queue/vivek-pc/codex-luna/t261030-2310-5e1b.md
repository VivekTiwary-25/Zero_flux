File: ui/CameraOverlay.kt, LiveOverlay only. A review reported: in the bottom Row the unweighted LiveControls measure before the weighted elbow Box, so long labels such as Malayalam live_pause and live_stop at 640x360 dp and font scale 1.3 crush the elbow label or clip Stop, and the Pause and Stop positions shift when isPaused toggles. In the top Row, the 104 sp RateReadout can clip its status line on a 360 dp tall screen at font scale 1.3. Fix so Pause and Stop always stay fully visible, at least 56 dp tall, and don't move when pausing or when the elbow label appears. Long labels may wrap or shrink, and must not clip. Don't change colours or the spec's rule that the rate is the biggest thing on screen.
This PC has the Android SDK at %LOCALAPPDATA%\Android\Sdk. You must compile and test: gradlew assembleDebug testDebugUnitTest in your reference copy, with sdk.dir in local.properties (forward slashes).
