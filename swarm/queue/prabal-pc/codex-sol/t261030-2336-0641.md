Today it forces Range(24, 30) with Camera2Interop without checking the phone offers it. A phone offering only (15,15), (15,30), (30,30) could then fail at capture time with a camera device error rather than ERROR_STREAM_CONFIG, so the fallback never runs and the camera stays dead. The Emergency screen now opens this camera by default, so it must never end up dead. Fix:
1. Choose the requested range from what the phone offers (CameraInfo supported frame rate ranges, or the Camera2 AE available ranges via Camera2CameraInfo): prefer the range with the highest lower bound that still reaches at least 24 at the top; if none, request nothing and log under CPR_CAM.
2. While a frame rate request is active, treat ANY camera error state, not just ERROR_STREAM_CONFIG, as a reason to rebind once with defaults.
3. A failure while unbinding during fallback must not abort the default bind.
Keep the rest, and don't change the public signature. Say what you checked.
