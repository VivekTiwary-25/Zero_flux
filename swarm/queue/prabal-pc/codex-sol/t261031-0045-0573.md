Real recordings are on the branch in tools/testdata/: real-cph2467-1..9.csv (real rescuers pushing to a 110/min metronome, about 20 frames a second, pose visibility about 0.95; columns t_ms,wrist_signal,elbow_deg,min_vis,latency_ms,frame_gap_ms,thermal_status,battery_temp_c,side,shoulder_x,shoulder_y,elbow_x,elbow_y,wrist_x,wrist_y,hip_x,hip_y,shoulder_vis,elbow_vis,wrist_vis,hip_vis). They have no human count and include non-pushing stretches. negative-cph2467-nobody.csv is a phone on a desk with nobody pushing: any confident rate from it is false. Today's engine (engine/RateEstimator.kt) gives plausible rates of 100-120 when it fires, but its checks reject most windows mid-push.

Using a faithful Python port of RateEstimator.kt outside this folder, plus tools/compare_signals.py: per recording, which check rejects each window (correlation, recent correlation, amplitude, density). Compare wrist minus hip, shoulder minus hip, shoulder alone and wrist alone, with windows of 3, 4, 5 and 6 seconds. Which signal and window gives the most beats with rates that agree over time, and how many false beats in the negative file? Say which single change would help most.
