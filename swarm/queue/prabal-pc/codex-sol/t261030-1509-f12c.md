Hard rule: flat translucent overlays only (CoachColors.CameraScrim). No blur, no Modifier.blur, no RenderEffect, no glass, since pose tracking must stay smooth. Avoid recomposing the whole overlay each frame.
Framing: a soft side-view kneeling body outline guide drawn with Canvas, in white at low alpha, turning teal when ready. Show the hint text for hintKey, and frame_ready when ready. Clay Start button with frame_start, enabled only when ready and shown only if showStart. Hints are for the holder, on screen only.
Countdown: one huge digit, nothing else.
Live: this is the screen the holder watches, readable at arm's length.
- A very large rate number with live_per_minute beneath. Teal when inRange is true, amber when false.
- A soft edge glow around the whole screen: teal in range, amber when off, none otherwise. Cross-fade over about 400 ms when it changes, and no pulsing.
- Elbow indicator: small icon and label, live_elbows_straight in teal or live_elbows_bent in amber. Hidden when elbowsBent is null.
- Time-left ring around the seconds left, from secondsLeft and totalSeconds.
- Pause/resume and stop as quiet flat buttons, at least 48dp, using live_pause, live_resume, live_stop.
Honesty rules, which matter more than looks:
- ratePerMinute null means no number is drawn, ever, not even the last value. Show a grey dash.
- tracking LOST: the dash in grey plus live_cant_see, and no teal or amber glow.
- tracking PAUSED: the dash plus live_paused in amber.
- tracking WARMING: the dash in grey and nothing else.
Nothing animates at rest except the ring's progress. Respect reduced motion by removing the cross-fade if ANIMATOR_DURATION_SCALE is 0.
