The Emergency mode has just been redesigned. Facts:
Practise teaches you: calm, with numbers, teal and amber, and a scorecard. Emergency talks you through it: panic-proof, one instruction, zero setup.
One tap on Emergency opens the camera in landscape, and at once starts the metronome and spoken steps. CPR never waits for the camera. Once the arms are visible, the same camera, analysis and coaching as Practise add spoken corrections, blended with the emergency steps (call 112, hands on chest, swap every 2 minutes, AED once). The screen shows one huge instruction word and no numbers: FASTER, GOOD, SLOWER or STRAIGHTEN ARMS, in the chosen language, inside a coral-red frame, with elapsed time in a corner. Call 112 is always visible, camera on too, and only opens the dialer: it never places a call. On-screen setup hint only: hold the phone from the side, or prop it on the floor facing the rescuer's side. It never blocks anything. If tracking fails, metronome and voice continue and the word area says "Keep pushing with the beat". It never makes up a number. On Stop (a deliberate hold) comes a "For the ambulance crew" summary: total CPR time, average rate, pauses, only measured values, a dash if unmeasured. There is no Helper camera button any more.
What is NOT proven: rate detection on a real person, and this new Emergency screen hasn't yet been run with a real person. Connected-call audio isn't proven. Quote no accuracy or latency numbers. Never suggest calling 112 in a test: use the dialer screen only and don't press call.

Update them to match. Use this line word for word in each of the demo scripts and in the pitch: "Practise teaches you. Emergency talks you through it." Show the Emergency beat in the demo as: one tap, beat and voice start at once, camera on, one word on screen, then hold to stop and show the "For the ambulance crew" summary. Remove the old statements that there is no helper camera, and the Start compressions step.
