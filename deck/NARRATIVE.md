# CPR Coach v4: the story

## The film in one line
Night, a railway platform. Someone has collapsed (never shown). A crowd stands and nobody knows what to do.
A phone comes out and is propped on the ground, its screen glowing teal. Someone kneels and pushes in rhythm.
Dawn: people sit around the same phone, learning together.

Act 1, setting and tension (slides 1-5): the platform, the frozen crowd, then the evidence of why (CPR rates, nobody trained, feedback needs a manikin).
Act 2, turning point (slides 6-9): the phone, what it sees, how it works, someone kneeling to push, how to push.
Act 3, resolution (slides 10-14): it speaks your language, the demo, honesty about limits, the tests, and dawn: what's next.

Continuity device: the same plain black phone with a blank teal-glowing screen is in every film frame.
Rhythm: F = FILM (full-bleed image, few words), E = EVIDENCE (ink background, data). Never more than two F in a row.

## Shot prompts
Shared rules (sent with every shot):
HARD RULES for every image: 16:9 landscape, 1920x1080 or larger, photographic film still, low-key light, shallow depth of field. One colour grade: blue-black shadows, teal highlights, a little warm amber. Leave the LEFT THIRD of the frame dark and simple (headline goes there). NO readable text, signs, letters, numbers or logos anywhere (no station signs, no screen text). NO visible faces: people only as silhouettes, from behind, or out of focus. Never show CPR on a realistic person; if hands pushing appear, they are out of focus and pushing on a plain dark cushion. The continuity device: one plain black smartphone with a soft teal-glowing blank screen (no UI, no text), propped upright on its side on the ground, appears in the frame.

- platform: wide night railway platform in India, wet concrete, distant blurred sodium and tube lights; on the right a small crowd seen from behind, out of focus, standing in a loose circle looking down at something hidden by their legs; the teal-glowing phone lies on the ground in the right foreground.
- crowd: low camera at knee height on the night platform: legs and shoes of several people standing still in a circle, out of focus; one person's hand hangs at their side holding the phone, its screen glowing teal; nobody is moving.
- kneel: low close shot on the night platform floor: the phone propped upright on its side, sharp, screen glowing teal toward camera-left; behind it, heavily out of focus, two hands with straight arms pushing down on a plain dark cushion on the floor; motion blur on the hands.
- circle: night platform, several people crouched in a circle seen from behind as dark silhouettes, leaning in toward the teal-glowing phone on the ground between them; rim light from the phone on shoulders.
- phone: macro close-up of the phone propped upright on its side on the wet platform floor, blank teal-glowing screen, bokeh of platform lights behind, reflections on the wet ground.
- dawn: same railway platform at dawn, warm amber sky low on the horizon, cool blue shadows; a few people seen from behind sitting on the platform floor, each with a plain dark cushion in front of them, gathered around the teal-glowing phone propped on the ground; calm.
