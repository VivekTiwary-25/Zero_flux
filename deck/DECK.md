# Deck: CPR Coach — Team Zero Flux — Anvathon 2026 pitch (presented live)

## 0. Dials
DENSITY 3 | MOTION 1 (static .pptx, cuts only) | VARIANCE 7

## 1. Format and engine
16:9 (13.333 × 7.5 in) | Engine: pptxgenjs → native, editable .pptx | Presented from a Windows laptop; PDF backup.
Identity inherits the app: ink background, teal = measured/good, amber = needs correcting, red = Emergency only.

## 2. Narrative arc (titles-only test)
1. CPR Coach (cover): a phone that watches your CPR and tells you what to fix
2. Only 1 in 10 people who collapsed got CPR from someone nearby
3. Under 2 in 100 people say they know CPR, and the skill fades in months
4. Feedback makes practice stick, but today it needs a sensor manikin
5. CPR Coach watches your arms and says what to fix
6. Camera to voice, every step runs on the phone
7. Practise teaches you; Emergency starts the beat in one tap
8. It coaches in ten Indian languages, in their own scripts
9. Live demo
10. It shows a dash, never a guess, for anything it can't see
11. 399 tests and 7 real recordings gate every change
12. Next: count it by hand, then test it with CPR trainers (close with action)

## 3. Signature element (exactly one)
**The push rhythm line** — the wrist up-and-down trace the app actually reads, drawn as one thin line.
It is flat on the problem slides (nobody is pushing), turns into a steady 110/min wave once CPR Coach
coaches, shows an amber slow stretch where the app says "Push faster", and breaks into a dash where the
app can't see. Sustained on cover, problem, solution, pipeline, honesty and close slides.

## 4. Palette and typography
- Ink #0E1418 base, text #EEF2F5, muted #93A3B5, hairlines #2A3642. One accent: teal #2DD4BF.
  Semantic only: amber #FBBF24 (correction), red #FF3B5C (Emergency, safety line).
- Display: Cambria (bold) for headlines — editorial, ships with Office, metric-safe in QA.
- Body: Calibri, kept short. Data and readouts: Consolas.
- Indic scripts: Nirmala UI (Windows).
- No glow, no grid, no gradients, no cards by default. Separation by whitespace and hairlines.

## 5. Archetype system (rotate; no archetype twice in a row)
cover · big number · two numbers · hairline table · image-led (screenshot or labelled illustration) ·
hand-built flow on the rhythm line · split comparison · typographic (scripts) · section divider ·
two plain lists · inline proof numbers · close with one action.

## 6. Charts and tables
One table only: no cell boxes, top hairlines, CPR Coach row highlighted in teal text, numbers mono.

## 7. Anti-patterns (BANNED)
Rounded cards as the default container · icon-in-a-circle on every heading · three/four equal boxes in a row
on more than one slide · glow effects · monitor grid · kicker label on every slide · body under 18 pt ·
straplines · empty "Thank you" · invented screenshots or numbers · AI copy (seamless, unlock, elevate).
